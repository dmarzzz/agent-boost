# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Agent Boost welcomes respectful, good-faith participation. Contributors and
maintainers are expected to be considerate, discuss ideas rather than people,
accept constructive feedback, and make room for differing backgrounds and levels
of experience.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized
attention, deliberate intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publishing
another person's private information without permission. Technical disagreement is
welcome; personal attacks are not.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, reviews, project chat,
events, and other spaces where someone is representing the project. It also applies
to private project communication when that conduct affects a person's ability to
participate safely.

## Reporting and enforcement

Maintainer `@dmarzzz` is responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard.
Report conduct privately through an established contact channel. If no private
channel is available, ask the maintainer for one without posting incident details.
For conduct on GitHub, you may also use GitHub's abuse-reporting tools.

Reports will be handled as privately and promptly as practical. The maintainer may
edit or remove contributions, issue a warning, temporarily restrict participation,
or permanently ban a participant, depending on the severity and pattern of the
conduct. Anyone involved in a report must recuse themselves from deciding its
outcome.

Good-faith reports and participation in an enforcement process must not result in
retaliation.
